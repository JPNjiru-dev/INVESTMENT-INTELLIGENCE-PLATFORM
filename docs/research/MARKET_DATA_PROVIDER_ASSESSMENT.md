# Market-Data Provider Assessment

**Assessment date:** 2026-11-17  
**Status:** Research only; no provider selected, connected, purchased, or approved.  
**Scope:** XAUUSD exposure, NAS100 exposure, FX majors, exchange futures, global equities/ETFs and crypto. Product coverage and legal permission are assessed separately. This is not legal advice or a complete contractual review.

## Executive assessment

There is no single source that can be recommended responsibly across the requested asset classes and use cases based on public information alone. The most promising diligence pair is (a) Databento for exchange-native futures historical/real-time research and explicit venue licensing; (b) a commercial cross-asset API such as Twelve Data or Massive for selected FX/commodity/crypto/index and equity coverage, only if exact product-level rights, geographic coverage, availability semantics, and correction policy are confirmed in writing. Nasdaq index products and CME exchange data may require direct or pass-through exchange agreements. Coinbase and Kraken APIs may be useful for venue-local crypto research but do not create rights to distribute a commercial multi-customer product.

The lower-cost retail API tiers are not automatically commercially usable. Vendor pages explicitly distinguish personal/internal from business and external distribution. For an application intended to be commercial, compare total cost including exchange fees, professional classification, non-display and customer-display licenses, data storage/caching, derived-output approvals, audit/reporting, region, and service levels. Do not select on list price alone.

**Gate 1 remains IN PROGRESS** because no legal entity, customer segment, geography, user count, exact data purpose, exchange entitlements, source-availability guarantees or written vendor quotes have been approved. No subscription, trial account, terms acceptance or data connection was made.

## Provider-specific evidence and limitations

### Databento

- **Products/history:** Advertises 45+ venues, 15+ years overall; for CME/CBOT/NYMEX/COMEX lists 16+ years, all futures/options/spreads, OHLCV from second through daily, trades, BBO, L2/L3. Historical usage price is dataset/schema/range dependent. Standard is shown at USD 199/month; Plus at USD 1,750/month with annual contract; Unlimited at USD 4,500/month with annual contract. Exact current quotes and product scopes must be checked before purchase.
- **Live access/rights:** Live real-time subscription and questionnaire; most commercial users need direct venue agreement or vendor-of-record licensing. Redistribution terms pass through per dataset; public docs say many datasets can be redistributed after 24 hours, subject to dataset-specific publisher terms. Plus advertises external distribution; dataset-specific legal status prevails.
- **Timestamps/availability:** Distinguishes exchange event (`ts_event`), capture receive (`ts_recv`) and gateway output (`ts_out`). Its capture timestamps have published hardware/PTP accuracy properties, while source event timestamps are not guaranteed to be accurate. Receive time is evidence of provider capture, not automatically entitlement/customer-access time.
- **Correction/revision:** Record/action schemas and source records exist; exact correction/cancel handling depends on venue/dataset. Confirm corrections, rebuild/backfill, retention, and customer point-in-time availability per dataset.
- **Limits/regions:** API and downloads depend on plan and license; regions/customer eligibility are not fully established from public product overview. Request written terms.
- **Official sources:** [Pricing](https://databento.com/pricing/), [Live data licensing](https://databento.com/docs/portal/live-data), [dataset catalog](https://databento.com/docs/portal/data-catalog), [timestamping](https://databento.com/docs/architecture/timestamping-guide), [timestamp conventions](https://databento.com/docs/conventions/common-fields-enums-types), [continuous contracts](https://databento.com/docs/examples/symbology/continuous).

### Twelve Data

- **Coverage/history:** Product catalog reports `Gold Spot / US Dollar XAU/USD`; on its listed commodity market page, daily/monthly history begins 1980-01-01 and intraday 1-minute to 8-hour begins 2020-01-09. Time series intraday offers multiple years “typically” and max 5,000 records per query; depth varies by instrument/interval. Its EOD support page says 100+ exchanges, EOD fields include OHLCV/reference/corporate actions, and intervals 1m to 1month, but EOD data itself is delayed to end-of-day.
- **Real-time/pricing/rate limits:** Personal Grow USD 79/mo, Pro USD 229/mo, Ultra USD 999/mo on public listing; commercial plans include Venture USD 499/mo monthly or USD 414/mo annual and Enterprise USD 1,099/mo monthly or USD 916/mo annual on shown listing. Add-ons and exchange charges extra. API/WS credits depend on endpoints/symbols; business page advertises specific credit packages, not unlimited. Basic is free with narrow credits and access; do not use free tier as evidence of commercial rights.
- **Rights:** Individual tiers are personal/internal, non-commercial. Business plans permit certain commercial internal/display rights subject to exchange licensing. Public support says non-US price data needs extra commercial approval; redistribution needs a separate agreement; derived analytics are allowed only if they cannot reconstruct raw data, with attribution. ASX has particular approval/registration restrictions. Exact terms need confirmation.
- **Timestamps/corrections:** Defaults vary by asset: crypto UTC, FX Australia/Sydney, stocks exchange local. User can request timezone. Public docs do not establish trustworthy point-in-time availability timestamps for every historical correction or a complete correction/revision event history. Vendor confirmation required.
- **Rates/region:** Requests and websocket subscriptions consume plan credits; exchange add-ons and geographies vary. Regional data restrictions and customer distribution rights require written confirmation.
- **Official sources:** [Pricing](https://twelvedata.com/pricing), [business pricing](https://twelvedata.com/pricing-business), [commercial vs personal](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/5332349-commercial-and-personal-usage), [terms](https://twelvedata.com/terms), [EOD description](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/12682324-end-of-day-pricing-market-data), [historical-depth guide](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/5656039-how-to-get-historical-prices), [timezone guide](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/5745849-timezones), [XAU/USD commodity symbol](https://twelvedata.com/exchanges/commodity?group=reference).

### Massive (formerly Polygon.io product branding)

- **Coverage/history:** Public API catalog covers US equities/ETFs, options, indices, futures (US venues), FX, crypto. Its documentation says listed US stock trades/quotes/bars go back to 2003-09-10; forex back to 2009-09-25; vendor gold `C:XAUUSD` begins 2009-09-27 but has a large historical gap July 2010–July 2013 and sparse quotes in later periods; several asset classes have plan-specific history and tick coverage. Futures flat files are US exchange markets and updated daily; they do not imply XAUUSD spot coverage. Listed US equities have ticks; forex aggregated bars are based on bid quotes, with `v` equal to quote count and not traded volume.
- **Real-time/pricing/rates:** Public personal pages show asset-specific subscription prices (for example currencies Starter at USD 49/month, indices Starter USD 49/month and Advanced USD 99/month; business/custom pricing separate). Plans distinguish EOD, 15-minute delayed and real-time. API quotas, data windows and access to trades/quotes differ by plan. These retail prices do not establish business-use total cost.
- **Rights:** Individual and business agreements differ; exchange terms (CME, Nasdaq/UTP, NYSE, OPRA) may be incorporated and separate subscriber agreements/fees apply. Business terms grant use within purchased entitlements for internal business, certain customer display and derived works, subject to third-party agreements and restrictions; systematic raw data feeds/redistribution may require additional rights. Do not conclude that an API tier grants platform/customer rights.
- **Timestamps/corrections:** Epoch timestamps represent UTC; units vary (REST aggregates ms, REST trades/quotes ns, websocket often ms, futures docs ns); unit errors can silently corrupt timestamps. US stock aggregate bars can be rebroadcast after late trades and corrected by REST; source explicitly describes end-of-day changes and late correction handling. This documented correction mechanism is stocks-specific; other markets require confirmation. Forex bars are quote-derived, bid-only.
- **Availability:** Some live bar history can be corrected later, but public docs do not establish exact historical entitled-availability timestamp for each correction. Fresh REST corrected data should not be backdated into older replay unless a correction event time is established.
- **Regions:** Exchange entitlement and professional classifications vary by jurisdiction and market. Confirm product/data access for intended customer geographies.
- **Official sources:** [API docs](https://massive.com/docs), [personal pricing](https://massive.com/pricing?product=currencies), [business terms](https://www.massive.com/legal/businesses-terms-of-service), [market data terms](https://massive.com/legal/market-data-terms-of-service), [US equity correction guide](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/why-am-i-receiving-a-late-aggregate-bar-through-massives-websockets), [historical depth](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/how-much-historical-stock-data-does-massive-have), [gold/forex source and gaps](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/categories/currencies), [forex bid-bar semantics](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/how-are-massives-forex-bars-built-and-what-do-v-and-n-mean-on-a-forex-bar), [timestamp units](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/what-timezone-are-massives-timestamps-in), [correction FAQ](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/how-much-does-massives-feeds-handle-canceled-trades).

### Nasdaq GIDS

- **Coverage:** Official index-value and ETP valuation product, including NDX, with real-time/EOD/historical offerings and constituent/weight data in related products. It is not the NQ futures market or Nasdaq-listed ETF tape.
- **Rights/pricing:** Firm data request and Global Data Agreement required for service. Public 2026 usage policy says external redistribution of GIDS real-time, EOD and historical data requires relevant agreement/service; new delayed GIDS is no longer offered since 2026-09-01. Benchmarking/product use has additional licensing requirements. Public fixed price is not listed; sales quote required.
- **Timestamp/history/corrections/limits:** Index page exposes currency and entitlement family; product uses feed-specific dissemination, schedules, and licensed access. A full historical depth by index/field and correction/availability event protocol must be confirmed. Exchange holidays, index calculation gaps and constituent restatements require vendor terms.
- **Official sources:** [NDX overview](https://indexes.nasdaq.com/Index/Overview/NDX), [GIDS product](https://www.nasdaq.com/products/global-indexes/data/gids), [index data policy update](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/TraderNews.aspx?id=DN2026-5), [2026 usage/distribution policy](https://assets.ctfassets.net/mx0rke14e5yt/6HcbUEVbkBsdmwwe4ekxgV/67a77206836f5005e609ecec4e05103f/Nasdaq_Global_Index_Data_Policies_08032026_Updates.pdf), [purchase/pricing process](https://nasdaqtrader.com/Trader.aspx?id=RealTimeIndexes).

### CME Group direct / authorized distributor

- **Coverage:** Primary exchange specifications for futures such as E-mini and Micro E-mini Nasdaq-100, and GC/MGC gold. Direct CME market data and broker/API feeds are not the same thing as a general XAUUSD spot/CFD feed.
- **Rights/pricing:** CME access is under agreement/license categories. Fees depend on exchange, professional status, display/non-display, distribution, historical, derived uses and application. The official 2026 fee list includes some fees but notes it is not exhaustive; obtain precise written quote and license. CME's market-data disclaimer expressly restricts AI/ML-related uses in contexts described there; applicability must be assessed by legal/vendor for exact feed and use, not assumed away.
- **Timestamp/history/corrections:** Venue feed schemas carry exchange-specific time, sequence, status and trade/cancel/update events; high fidelity is possible through licensed feeds, but effective use depends on exact product/schema and rights. Confirm historical corrections and customer-visible time semantics.
- **Official sources:** [CME license data](https://www.cmegroup.com/market-data/license-data.html), [information policy](https://www.cmegroup.com/market-data/license-data/information-policies.html), [derived data](https://www.cmegroup.com/market-data/browse-data/derived-data.html), [2026 fee list](https://www.cmegroup.com/market-data/files/january-2026-market-data-fee-list.pdf), [market data disclaimer](https://www.cmegroup.com/trading/market-data-explanation-disclaimer.html).

### Crypto direct venues: Kraken and Coinbase

- **Kraken:** Public endpoints expose trade timestamps, publication timestamps, MIC, price and quantity. Official downloadable OHLCVT and time-and-sales archives provide full venue-specific history with quarterly updates; the OHLC REST endpoint itself is only 720 candles. Current REST rate limits and product terms must be checked for exact application. Exchange data does not equal composite crypto. Official: [trade API](https://docs.kraken.com/api/docs/rest-api/get-post-trade), [OHLCVT archive](https://support.kraken.com/articles/360047124832-downloadable-historical-ohlcvt-open-high-low-close-volume-trades-data), [trade archive](https://support.kraken.com/articles/360047543791-downloadable-historical-market-data-time-and-sales-), [720 candle limitation](https://support.kraken.com/ca/articles/advanced-api-faq).
- **Coinbase:** Official market-data terms state data may be inaccurate/delayed, and absent express written consent prohibit external redistribution/display of raw market data or derived works. Market-data API surface is venue/product-specific. Public docs do not establish commercial application storage/retention, correction history, or global customer rights; written consent is needed for use beyond permitted scope. Official: [market data terms](https://www.coinbase.com/legal/market_data).

## Recommendation

Do not select or connect a vendor yet. Conduct a rights-first written RFI after the owner defines product geography, internal-vs-customer display, expected user/device counts, analytic/AI use, retention, real-time needs and permitted redistribution. A plausible architecture is a narrow multi-source portfolio: exchange-native futures via a licensed provider; separate index entitlement if NDX value is required; one commercial multi-asset source for validated global EOD plus carefully selected FX/crypto; and explicit provider-specific broker CFD mappings only if a CFD is actually the intended instrument. Choose based on source quality, contractual rights and total cost.

### Required RFI questions

1. Exact provider source, publisher, venues, symbols, quote/trade sides, instrument definitions and global coverage, including XAUUSD and NDX/NQ/MNQ.
2. Historical depth by bars/trades/quotes, intervals, missing-data map and endpoint plan limits; tick precision and size semantics.
3. Real-time/delayed/EOD status per field and region; SLA, incident status feed, latency percentiles and service geography.
4. Timestamp units, timestamp origin (event/participant/vendor receive/publication/customer), timezone, clock quality, session/date labels and daylight-saving rule.
5. Corrections, cancellations, restatements, late trades, current REST vs delivered archive, source revision IDs, correction publication times and as-of replay support.
6. Exact acquired/internal/display/non-display/derived/AI/ML/benchmarking/redistribution rights, including customer/device/user counts, audience, territories, attribution, extractability, APIs, downloadable files, training and outputs.
7. Storage, caching, backup, derived retention, deletion after termination, audit/reporting duties and historical retention.
8. Required exchange agreements, subscriber classifications, direct licenses, professional/nonprofessional definitions, fees and whether vendor is a vendor of record.
9. Itemized recurring, setup, exchange, user, display, non-display, geographic, API/WS, overage, historical re-download and termination costs; taxes/currency and annual increases.
10. Data residency, regional access, sanctions/export/geographic restrictions, customer geography restrictions and subprocessors.

No answer from an API test or free tier replaces contractual confirmation. Record executed version/term and human approval in `DATA_RIGHTS_REGISTER.md`; until then entitlement is absent and access fails closed.
