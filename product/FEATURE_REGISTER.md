## Gate 1 discoveries — comparative investment assessment

Scales below are planning estimates (low / medium / high), not vendor quotes or approved scope. Decision value describes expected reduction in bad decisions/research ambiguity; no feature should be built solely because it is listed.

| Feature | Expected decision value | Engineering cost | Data cost | Main risk | Dependencies / sequencing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F-009 Market-data quality and source reliability | High: prevent stale, missing, anomalous or incomplete observations from entering analysis | Medium | Low with existing feeds; medium/high for independent benchmarks | False confidence in quality score; false alarms | Approved source, session expectation, freshness thresholds; before any signal/portfolio use |
| F-010 Correction and revision lineage | High: preserve point-in-time replay and explain backfill differences | Medium | Medium: retention, correction history and re-downloads | Backdating later corrections leaks future knowledge | Source correction IDs/times, retention rights, immutable store; prerequisite for backtesting |
| F-011 Versioned instrument master and calendar | Very high: prevent wrong units, hours, DST, symbol mapping and contract spec | High | Low/medium for official schedules; high if commercial calendar data purchased | Wrong calendar or instrument metadata invalidates all calculations | Gate 1; build before sizing/session strategies; primary exchange schedule sources first |
| F-012 Corporate-action and point-in-time security master | High for equity/ETF screening and portfolio returns | High | High: global actions, delisted coverage and history often paid | Survivorship, restatement, adjustment errors | Gate 1 rights; Gate 4, before historical fundamental backtests |
| F-013 Futures expiry and roll lineage | High for futures returns, expiry safety and replay integrity | Medium/high | Medium/high: expiry definitions and granular history/contract data rights | Roll rule creates synthetic bias or delivery exposure | Gate 1 venue specs/calendars; before NQ/GC futures research |
| F-014 Cross-provider discrepancy monitor | Medium/high: diagnose symbol/timestamp/venue/adjustment divergence | High | High: two independently licensed sources and overlap | Compares non-equivalent observations; costs/rights multiply | At least two approved like-for-like feeds; defer until business case and rights pass |
| F-015 Research lineage/run manifest | Very high: reproduce and audit every result | Medium | Low incremental data, medium storage over time | Incomplete lineage makes reproducibility illusory | Start with Gate 2 deterministic run engine; must capture rights and snapshots |
